### General Direction of our Project

#### Hardware Voice Version (My Pick)
ESP32-S3 rides on the glasses you're wearing, with an INMP441 (mic model can be finalized later) near your ear to pick up your voice, or the chip connects straight to the AirPods and the answer plays right in your ear.
